The question

Arrhythmias start with the electrical identity of single cells

Gene regulatory networks set each cardiomyocyte's electrical identity: which ion channels, calcium-handling proteins, and connexins it expresses. Those links have mostly been worked out one gene at a time.

My lab will work at the level of the whole network. The long-term goal is to understand how these networks establish and maintain cardiomyocyte electrophysiology, and how changes to them cause pathogenic remodeling.

Tools

Two platforms I built

DynaGRIN

Uses gene regulatory networks inferred from scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq data and perturbs them in silico to rank the transcription factors that control cell state. Applied to the heart field, it predicted 32 TFs that drive atrial vs. ventricular identity.

CARBONITE

Live calcium imaging followed by immunostaining of the same cells, giving function and molecular identity cell by cell. Works across cardiomyocytes, neural cells, lymphatic endothelial cells, and differentiating mesoderm. Preprint →
