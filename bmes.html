The poster in 60 seconds

Gene networks build each cardiomyocyte's electrical identity

The links between gene regulatory networks and cardiomyocyte electrophysiology have been worked out one gene at a time. My lab will work at the network level.

Area 1 How the program is built

Gene regulatory network control of cardiomyocyte electrical identity

model → predict → validate

Mutations in early, dosage-sensitive heart-forming TFs (MEF2C, PURA, RREB1, NR2F2, HEY2) may cause cell-autonomous rhythm disturbances independent of structural malformation.
Area 2 Why individual cells fail

Single-cardiomyocyte determinants of electrical vulnerability

measure → encode → constrain

Covariance among regulators and effectors, measured in thousands of single cells, may predict which cells diverge under perturbation, including delayed remodeling from cancer therapies.
