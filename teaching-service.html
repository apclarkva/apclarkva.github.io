Teaching research

Teaching AI ethics alongside technical skills

With Groves, Wintruba, and Saucerman (UVA BME), I helped add social and ethical AI topics to a computational biomedical engineering course. Student engagement went up with no loss of technical learning. We presented the results at the 2026 ASEE Annual Conference.
